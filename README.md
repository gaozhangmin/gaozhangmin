### 🎬 Meet BoxPlayer

**Your Media. Your Way. Everywhere.**

[🌐 Website](https://boxplayer.cc) · [🍎 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/boxplayer/id6739804060)

BoxPlayer transforms scattered media across cloud storage, NAS, media servers, and local devices into a unified entertainment experience.

**✨ Highlights**

- 🎨 **Personalized Home** — Build your own media dashboard with customizable sections and beautiful poster walls.
- ☁️ **Cloud Storage** — Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Aliyun Drive, 115, 123 Cloud Drive, and more.
- 🖥️ **Media Servers & NAS** — Plex, Emby, Jellyfin, SMB, NFS, WebDAV, and AList.
- 🎞️ **Powerful Playback** — 4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, Blu-ray ISO, multiple playback engines, and advanced subtitles.
- 🌍 **Cross-Platform** — iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, Windows, Android, and Android TV.
- 🔄 **Apple Ecosystem** — Seamless iCloud sync across Apple devices.
- 📚 **Beyond Video** — Music libraries, particle music visualizations, and EPUB/PDF reading.
- 🎯 **Trakt Integration** — Sync watch history, ratings, and favorites.
